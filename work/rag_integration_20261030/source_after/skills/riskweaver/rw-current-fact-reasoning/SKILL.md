# Current facts govern the attack

1. Identify the separately represented ego and CURRENT active background targets.
   Read supplied longitudinal/lateral labels and radial distance-change signs.
   A current closing motion is not an attack-added benefit.
2. Plan interaction against time-aligned possible EGO FUTURES, not its current
   position. Forecasts are hypotheses, never executed outcomes or exact policy truth.
3. Anchor time, recipe event time and actual attack validity differ. A 3-second
   endpoint does not prove an effect during a 1-second lease. Maintain one episode's
   target/time continuity when supported; a recipe named rolling is not evidence
   that a previous accepted attack exists.
4. Copy identities and current catalog IDs exactly. Distinguish min-to-min distance
   differences from SAME-TIME differences. Read explicit extrema sets: a larger
   finite background margin is larger, not lower. Null is not zero or safety.
   Avoid superlatives without a named metric and a supported comparison set.
5. Skills supply rules. Current deterministic facts outrank historical anecdotes.
   Useful validated current candidates do not require historical success cases.
   Rank feasible supported probes when appropriate; state uncertainties rather
   than inventing measurements or claiming inevitable/observed collisions.
6. Choose discrete strategy, target and sparse intent only. Do not output dense
   controls or modify diffusion solver, risk definitions, safety gates or u.
   Use current validators; old failed cases cannot restore removed road/TTC gates.

Keep the existing response schema. Each ranked reason must be concrete and based
on supplied facts. A valid schema/reference is not proof that every sentence is
correct: give restrained, checkable claims and preserve unknowns.
